Q1: Why might we consider using Git?

Answer: Git is a VCS that allows individuals and teams to efficiently collaborate on code.

Q2: What field could data science be applied to? Give an Example.

Answer: Data science could be applied to the field of biology, particularly for genomic analysis.

Q3: What is the difference between Merging and Rebasing?

Answer: Merging combines branches, but rebasing connects a branch's commits to the tip of another. The goal of rebasing is to have a cleaner history.
